
\section{Timing and Synchronization}
\label{sec:timing}
Timing and synchronization are distributed via the \gls{dts} over OS2 single-mode fiber using 1000Base-BX optics.
As specified for FD timing endpoints in EDMS 2580777, the endpoint optics transmit at 1550\,nm and receive at 1490\,nm;
the associated timing-master optics are complementary. The downlink is continuous and endpoints
must recover cleanly from interruptions. Uplink provides a minimal protocol subset for status and delay tuning.

\subsection{Clocking}
The reference tick is 62.5\,MHz. Endpoint clock jitter shall be $<180$\,ps RMS. Timestamping uses the \gls{dts} epoch
and must be aligned across endpoints within 5\,ns at the firmware interface. Endpoint logic must compensate fixed and
variable delays introduced by front-end processing so the timestamp represents the time of the physics signal at the sensor.

\subsection{Alignment Procedure (example)}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Receive downlink; verify lock and phase.
  \item Apply programmable delay tap to align PPS \& ToD.
  \item Issue epoch reset; confirm timestamp continuity.
  \item Log residual skew and jitter; raise alarm if out of tolerance.
\end{enumerate}
