
\section{Data Formats and Protocols}
\label{sec:dataformats}

\subsection{Packetization and Transport}
Frames are sent from \gls{daphne} to the \gls{daq} over 10\,GbE using UDP with jumbo frames (MTU $\sim$9000~B).
Multiple frame blocks may be aggregated per UDP packet to optimize bandwidth efficiency. The control plane is strictly
separated and shall not be multiplexed with readout traffic.

\subsection{Frame Structure (Simple Self-Trigger Mode)}
Each frame comprises 64-bit words with MSB at bit~63 and LSB at bit~0. Words~0--8 form the header with identifiers,
time-stamp and trigger context, while words~9--$N$ carry the waveform payload (14-bit ADC samples densely packed and
potentially split across word boundaries).

\subsection{Decoding Notes}
Split ADC fields (e.g., \texttt{ADC([13:4],n)} and \texttt{ADC([3:0],n)}) must be reassembled to recover the full 14-bit
sample. A machine-readable decoder map (CSV) accompanies this document to enable software auto-generation of unpackers.
