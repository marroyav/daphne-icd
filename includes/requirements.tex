
\section{Interface Requirements and Budgets}
\label{sec:reqs}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.16}
\begin{longtable}{@{}p{2.1cm}p{9.5cm}p{2.2cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{ID} & \textbf{Requirement} & \textbf{Type} \\\midrule
REQ-DATA-001 & The PDS--\gls{daq} link shall use 10\,GbE optical (10GBase-SR) over OM3/OM4 with LC connectors. & Physical \\
REQ-DATA-002 & Data shall be transmitted using UDP over Ethernet with jumbo frames (MTU $\sim$9000~B). & Protocol \\
REQ-DATA-003 & Maximum sustained payload throughput per DAPHNE link shall not exceed 9.5\,Gb/s (including protocol overheads). & Performance \\
REQ-DATA-004 & Packet loss (steady-state) shall be $<10^{-12}$ BER equivalent; dropped-packet flagging must be explicit in the packet header. & Reliability \\
REQ-DATA-005 & Time-stamping shall use the \gls{dts} epoch and be identical across endpoints to within 5\,ns at the firmware interface. & Timing \\
REQ-DATA-006 & Clock jitter at the endpoint shall be $<180$\,ps RMS integrated over the specified band. & Timing \\
REQ-CTRL-001 & Control/config/monitoring shall use dedicated 1\,GbE optical links, logically separated from data. & Physical \\
REQ-CTRL-002 & All \gls{ccm}-initiated commands shall receive an acknowledgement with status code and timestamp. & Protocol \\
REQ-CTRL-003 & Firmware update operations shall expose a software flag indicating path unavailability during reprogramming. & Operations \\
REQ-OPS-001 & Auto-recovery must attempt link re-sync and timing re-lock within $\le$\,10\,ms before escalating to \gls{ccm}. & Operations \\
REQ-SEC-001 & Control plane shall implement role-based access and per-endpoint authentication. & Security \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
