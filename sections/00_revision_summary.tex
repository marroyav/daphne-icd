\section*{Revision 8 change summary}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Revision 8 change summary}

The controlled revision 7 baseline is the 10 June 2026 source revision. This proposed
revision 8 incorporates the DAQ--SC configuration boundary developed with the new PDS
OPC-UA variable and control-policy files and the SC--PDS ICD draft. It does not transfer
DAQ configuration responsibility to SC. Monitoring or archiving a value does not confer
write authority over that value.

Configuration ownership & CCM described as a common configuration and monitoring path; the DAQ--SC write boundary was not explicit. & DAQ owns all values that determine acquired data. SC owns equipment lifecycle, power, availability, monitoring, protection, and safe-state actions. DPS protection remains authoritative. \\
Configuration source & DAPHNE Protobuf operations were named, without identifying the DAQ configuration source of truth. & The DAQ data-taking model remains versioned in the DUNE-DAQ framework and \texttt{daphnemodules}. The OPC-UA model provides a controlled mapping and must not become an independent SC copy of the DAQ schema. \\
Control architecture & Direct Protobuf CCM use and possible coexistence of controllers were described. & DAQ and SC are separate authenticated OPC-UA clients of one neutral PDS gateway. The gateway is the sole routine board-service client, enforces field ownership, and serializes accepted mutations. \\
Bias control & Numerical bias setting and bias power/permission were not separated. & DAQ owns the numerical bias target used for data taking. SC owns bias enable, equipment readiness, and operational permits; DPS may independently inhibit or remove permission. \\
Run and recovery state & Acknowledgement and generic recovery were required. & Every mutation carries role, scope, run type, configuration revision/hash, and correlation ID. Safe-state action invalidates the active DAQ configuration; DAQ must apply and verify a fresh configuration before data taking resumes. \\
LCM calibration & Synchronous calibration behavior was described without a DAQ--SC split. & DAQ/PDS owns pulse settings and sequence execution; SC owns external power, availability, health, alarms, and protective inhibit. \\
Timing optics/startup & Timing optics and startup wording were ambiguous. & Endpoint optics are TX 1550\,nm/RX 1490\,nm with complementary master optics; timing lock enables configuration, while data transmission remains separately controlled by run state. \\
Verification & Interface verification did not test role ownership or concurrency. & Revision 8 adds schema traceability, authorization-negative, concurrency, permit, restart/recovery, monitoring-quality, LCM, scale, and security tests. \\

The named gateway methods and variable paths in this revision are an interface proposal.
They become normative only after the OPC-UA NodeSet, DAQ-to-OPC-UA mapping, permissions,
state guards, limits, and conformance tests are jointly approved and versioned.
