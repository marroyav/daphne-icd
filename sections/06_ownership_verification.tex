\section{Configuration-boundary verification}
\label{sec:ownership-verification}

Approval of the revision 8 control interface requires the following end-to-end tests. A
test passes only when the OPC-UA result, gateway audit record, board readback, DAQ run
state, and SC equipment state agree. Negative results shall identify the rejected field,
role, state guard, or permit; a transport acknowledgement alone is not evidence that a
setting was applied.

CFG-10 & At the planned board/channel scale, verify reconnect behavior, subscription load, command latency, rate limiting, authorization, certificate handling, and complete audit attribution. No loss of protection priority or cross-role ownership is permitted. & Load/security report and acceptance thresholds. \\
